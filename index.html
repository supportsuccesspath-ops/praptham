Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect:

  • Account information such as your name, email address, mobile number, password, gender, date of birth, and account status.
  • Matrimonial profile information that you choose to provide, including location, education, occupation, family details, preferences, personal description, and astrological information.
  • Photos and horoscope documents or entries that you upload to your profile.
  • Search activity, profile views, contact-detail unlocks, and related account-credit activity.
  • Technical and security information needed to operate the service, such as authentication session data, device or request information, IP address, and audit records.

How We Use Information

  • Create and authenticate your account.
  • Build, display, search, and manage matrimonial profiles according to your settings.
  • Review and approve registrations, profile changes, photos, and horoscope submissions.
  • Provide contact-detail access controls and record related transactions.
  • Protect the service against fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, and security incidents.
  • Respond to support requests and maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the service.

Profile Visibility and Sharing

Information in your approved profile may be visible to other registered users so they can search for and review compatible profiles. Contact details are restricted by the service’s access-control rules and are not intended to be publicly indexed.

We may share information with service providers that help us host, operate, secure, or maintain the service. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or to investigate suspected fraud or abuse.

Photos and Uploaded Content

Uploaded photos and horoscope content are stored with your profile and may be displayed after review and approval. Do not upload content that you do not have permission to share. You can request deletion of uploaded content through the Contact section below.

Security

We use access controls, authentication, input validation, secure password handling, approval workflows, and audit logging designed to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Retention and Deletion

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service, meet legal or security requirements, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. You may request account or data deletion by contacting us. Some records may be retained where required for legal, fraud-prevention, security, or audit purposes.

Your Choices

You may review and update profile information through the application, control what you submit, and request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information. You may stop using the service at any time. Deleting the application does not automatically delete your account, so contact us for account deletion.

Children

Praptham is intended for adults who are legally eligible to use a matrimonial service. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us so we can investigate and delete it where appropriate.

Third-Party Services

The application may use platform or infrastructure services needed for hosting, authentication, storage, image selection, and opening web links. Those services may process information under their own privacy policies. We do not control the privacy practices of independent third parties.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the service or legal requirements change. We will update the effective date above and, where appropriate, provide additional notice in the application.
